FAQ

  • What language do they speak in Maldives?

    +

    While the national language is Dhivehi, most Maldivians can speak English with no problems.

  • Is it safe to swim in the Maldives?

    +

    Swimming in the Maldives can be a wonderful experience, but it's important to be aware of the potential risks and take precautions. Avoid swimming in areas with strong currents, high waves or dangerous underwater conditions.

  • Is Maldives a country?

    +

    Yes, the Maldives is a country. It is an independent island nation located in the Indian Ocean.
